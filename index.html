What BizScore PH actually does

Five capabilities, described honestly — this is where we are today, not a finished promise.

Document intelligence

Upload registration, tax, permit, banking, and financial documents. Extraction runs automatically and records a confidence level for every field it reads — nothing is presented as certain when it isn't.

Verification & reconciliation

Figures you declare are compared against what your documents and interview answers say. Matches get verified; mismatches are flagged clearly and resolved by a human reviewer — never silently auto-decided.

Adaptive AI interview

A short, text-based conversation that asks only about what's genuinely missing or inconsistent — not a generic questionnaire. Your answers feed straight back into the same verification process.

Configurable scoring

A transparent, rules-based score across weighted pillars — legal & identity, financial strength, repayment & debt, business stability, data consistency, and sector — not an opaque machine-learned black box.

Financing readiness

A shareable summary of how ready your business profile is for financing conversations — an input for lending partners to consider, not a loan decision or an approval.

Please read this

BizScore PH provides an indicative business credibility and financing-readiness assessment based on available information. It is not an official credit bureau score and does not guarantee financing approval. Final eligibility, financing amount, pricing, and approval are determined by the financing provider.

AI assists the process — reading documents, asking interview questions, and computing the score — but it does not make the final call on anything material. Conflicting or uncertain information is flagged and resolved by a human reviewer, and every figure in your profile carries its source and verification status so you can see exactly where it came from.
